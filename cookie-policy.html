1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They help the site work, remember your choices, and let us understand how the site is used. Some cookies are set by us, and some are set by other companies whose services appear on our pages.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies to keep the website working properly, to measure how visitors use it so we can improve it, and to show content from other services, such as embedded maps.

3. The cookies we use

The main types are:

  • Strictly necessary cookies. These are needed for the website to function. The site cannot work properly without them, so they do not require consent.

Some cookies are set by other companies whose content we use. We do not control these cookies. You can read how those companies use cookies in their own policies, for example the Google Privacy and Terms pages for our embedded maps.

5. Managing your cookies

You can accept or reject non essential cookies through our cookie banner [if you use a consent tool, name it here]. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. If you block some cookies, parts of the site may not work as well.
